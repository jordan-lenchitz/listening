# listening to *a cappella* singing: an ecological approach

my dissertation argues that certain [pitches of spectral fission](https://youtu.be/m1SKJ5ZA5HI?si=vIj75nll8JCKxYak&t=100) are necessarily perceived by listeners who have [learned to take up their affordance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibsonian_ecological_theory_of_development). multi-pitch trackers tend to find these only as a side effect, whence my addition of an affordance field which shows where their perception is structurally supported.
